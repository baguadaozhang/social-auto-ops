# Facebook 公共主页文案模板（填空版）

> 用法：把 `[方括号]` 的内容替换成公司真实信息；不确定的先留空，别编。
> 原则：像一家真工厂在说话，不像广告；少用 "best/cheapest/No.1"，多用具体数字、流程、交期。

---

## 1. 主页名称（3 选 1）

| 方案 | 名称 | 适合 |
|---|---|---|
| A | `[Brand] Sanitary Ware` | 品牌名清晰，搜索友好 |
| B | `[Brand] Bathroom Solutions` | 产品线较宽（柜/淋浴/龙头） |
| C | `[Company] Bathroom OEM & Projects` | 主打 OEM 与酒店工程 |

> 名称 ≤75 字符；不要写 "China Cheap Factory"；不要堆关键词；尽量与域名/Logo 一致。

## 2. @用户名（3 选 1）

- `@[brand]`
- `@[brand]bath`
- `@[brand]global`

> 用户名一旦定了尽量不改；被占用就加 `hq` / `official`，不要加一串数字。

## 3. 类别

首选：**Building Materials**（建材，B2B 更准）
备选：**Bathroom Remodeling**（更偏家装消费者）/ **Manufacturer**（工厂）

> 类别影响推荐人群；做进口商/工程就选建材或制造商，不要选"家具店"。

## 4. 简介（Short description，≤255 字符）

**版本 A（产品导向）**
> Bathroom vanities, stone tops, showers & faucets for importers, distributors and hotel projects. OEM/ODM from drawing to packaging. Based in [City], China. Message us with your drawings.

**版本 B（能力导向）**
> A bathroom manufacturer in [City], China. Stone vanity tops, bathroom furniture, shower systems and faucets. OEM/ODM, hotel projects, export packing. Send your drawing for a quote.

## 5. 关于（About，长版）

**版本 A（工厂/OEM）**
> [Brand] is a bathroom products manufacturer based in [City], China, serving importers, distributors and hotel projects since [Year].

> We specialize in stone vanity tops, bathroom furniture, shower systems and faucets, with OEM/ODM support from drawing and sample to packaging and container loading. Our factory covers [X] m² and produces around [X] units per month.

**版本 B（项目/工程）**

>
> Send us the bathroom layout and room count; we will flag the risks before quoting.

## 7. 行动按钮（CTA）

- 首选：**Send Message**（B2B 询盘直接进 Messenger，回复快）
- 备选：**Learn More** → 官网产品页
- 不建议：**Shop Now**（没有独立站电商就别用）

## 8. 主页前 9 格（Grid）

| 位置 | 内容 | 说明 |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 品牌封面图 | Logo + 一句话定位 + 主营产品 |
| 2 | 工厂/车间 | 证明是工厂，不是贸易商 |
| 3 | 产品主图（浴室柜） | 白底或场景，统一色调 |
| 4 | 质检特写 | 卡尺/塞尺/灯检，体现质量 |
| 5 | 酒店项目 | 现场图，打码客户信息 |
| 6 | 包装/装柜 | 护角、装柜照，体现交付 |
| 7 | 认证/标准 | 证书局部，不要整张糊图 |
| 8 | 团队/展会 | 真人出镜，增加可信度 |
| 9 | CTA 图卡 | "Send us your drawing" + 邮箱/WhatsApp |

> 前 9 格决定客户点进来 3 秒内的判断；宁可少发，不要杂图。

## 10. Messenger 回复模板（英文）

**问价格**
> Thanks for reaching out. To quote accurately, could you send: 1) drawing or model reference, 2) quantity per SKU, 3) target port, 4) packaging preference? We'll come back within one business day.

**要目录**
> Happy to share. Are you sourcing for a private-label program or a project? I'll send the relevant catalog and MOQ sheet.

**问 MOQ**
> Standard MOQ is [X] per SKU for stock colors; [Y] for custom finishes. Sample lead time is [X] days, production [X] days after sample approval.

**酒店项目**
> For projects, please send the bathroom layout and room count. We'll check as-built risks (wall alcove, floor drain, mirror height) before quoting.

**要样品**
> We can make a pre-production sample. Sample fee is [X], refundable against the first order. Please send logo files and packaging artwork/dieline if it is private label.

---

## 11. 不要写的东西

- "Cheapest price / best quality / No.1 factory"——没有信息量，且容易引起反感；
- 未经授权的客户 Logo、酒店名、项目照片；
- 证书上没覆盖的产品或型号；
- 每条帖子都放外链（前两周尤其不要）；
- 夸大产能/面积/年限——客户参观工厂时会对不上。
