## 第 1 周：质量与交期

### 1. 石材色差（Quality）· 周二
**视觉**：车间 5000K 灯下排开的石板 / 批次照片墙
**正文**：
We inspect every batch of stone when it arrives, not just a spot check at the end. For vanity tops the killer is shade variation: two slabs that look identical in the yard can look like different stones under bathroom light. So we lay the panels out under 5000K light and grade them before cutting. We also photograph every batch and keep the images with the job file. When a buyer says container two doesn't match container one, we can pull the photos instead of arguing.

How do you handle shade variation on natural stone tops?

### 2. 第二件样品（OEM）· 周四
**视觉**：生产线上的样品 / 样品间对比
**正文**：
For OEM bathroom furniture, the first sample is rarely the problem. It's sample number two. The first one gets made by the senior team with extra care. The second one, made by whoever is free that week, tells you what the factory is actually like. We now ask for the second sample to be produced during normal production, on the line, not in the sample room. It costs a week and some scrap, but the conversation afterward is much shorter.

Do you insist on a production-line sample, or is that asking too much?

#OEM #bathroomfurniture #sourcing

### 3. 交期从样品开始（Delivery）· 周六
**视觉**：PI 上的里程碑截图（打码）/ 日历
**正文**：
Lead time conversations usually go wrong at the sample stage, not in production. We quote 35 days from PO, but that assumes the buyer's sample is approved and the packaging artwork is signed off. If those slip a week, the shipment slips a week, and nobody remembers whose week it was. So we put sample approval and artwork approval on the PI as separate dated milestones. It looks fussy on paper, but it ends the "you promised 35 days" conversation before it starts.

How do you handle buyer-side delays?

#leadtime #export #supplychain

## 第 2 周：包装与项目

### 4. 包装的隐形亏损（Packing）· 周二
**视觉**：护角 / 装柜照片
**正文**：
Packing is where we lose money quietly. Cartons that pass a drop test in the factory still arrive with crushed corners, because the loading crew stacks three pallets high and the bottom one carries all of it. Two changes helped us: corner protectors on every mirror carton, and one photo of the loaded container taken from the door side. That photo has ended more claims than any packing specification we have written.

What is the one packing detail you will not ship without?

#exportpacking #qualitycontrol #shipping

### 5. 酒店项目的 20mm（Project）· 周四
**视觉**：酒店浴室现场 / 台面切割
**正文**：
One hotel project had 180 rooms and four bathroom layouts. The drawings were fine. The problem was the vanity for room type C was 20 mm wider than the wall alcove in the as-built drawing. We shipped the counters slightly oversize and trimmed them on site against a template. That decision had to be made before loading, not after.

For those who do hospitality work: do you price as-built verification into the job, or leave it as the client's risk?

#hoteldesign #bathroomdesign #hospitality

### 6. 认证会改产品（Compliance）· 周六
**视觉**：证书 + 阀芯/起泡器零件
**正文**：
CUPC and Watermark are not paperwork exercises, they change the product. A faucet that passes CUPC has a specific aerator and flow restrictor combination, and the cartridge and body material have to match the listing. Change one supplier component without re-testing and the certificate no longer covers what you ship. We keep the approved BOM next to the certificate so purchasing cannot swap parts quietly.

How do you keep certifications valid across suppliers?

#CUPC #watermark #faucets

## 第 3 周：细节与交付

### 7. 公差与木材变形（Quality）· 周二
**视觉**：湿度箱 / 门缝塞尺
**正文**：
Tolerances are the least glamorous thing we deal with, and buyers usually ask about them last. Wood moves, and a vanity in a humid bathroom moves more. We hold door gaps within 1.5 mm and check them after 48 hours in the humidity chamber, not straight off the line. A door that is perfect at 9 am can be tight by the next morning.

What tolerance do you actually inspect on arrival, not just on the drawing?

#bathroomfurniture #qualitycontrol #manufacturing

---

### 8. 装柜照片（Logistics）· 周四
**视觉**：从柜门方向拍的装柜照
**正文**：
We take one photo of every loaded container from the door side before sealing. Not for marketing—for claims. If a carton arrives crushed, the photo shows whether it was loaded that way, shifted in transit, or damaged at destination. It also shows the loading pattern: how many rows, whether corner protectors were used, whether the last pallet was braced. Five minutes per container, and it changes the conversation from "your packing failed" to "here is what left the factory".

Do you document loading, or only packing?

#logistics #exportpacking #supplychain

### 9. 报价前问三件事（Sales）· 周六
**视觉**：图纸 + 数量表 + 包装示意
**正文**：
Before we quote a vanity, we ask three things: the drawing with dimensions, the target quantity per SKU, and how it will be packed (pallet or carton, mixed or single). Without those, two suppliers can quote the same model and mean completely different products. The drawing decides the material yield, the quantity decides the setup cost, and the packing decides the freight. Most "cheap" quotes become expensive at one of those three points.

What do you ask before you quote?

#sourcing #OEM #bathroomvanity

## 第 4 周：产品与信任

### 10. 台面边型（Product detail）· 周二
**视觉**：3mm vs 5mm 边型特写
**正文**：
Edge profile is a small detail that decides how a vanity looks in a hotel bathroom. A 3 mm eased edge reads sharp and modern; a 5 mm bullnose hides chips better and feels softer on the hand. On stone tops, the profile also changes how the top meets the cabinet and how water runs off. We cut a sample of each profile before the first production run, because a drawing cannot show how light hits the edge.

Do you specify profile by name, or by sample?

#bathroomdesign #hoteldesign #stonefabrication

---

### 11. 私标项目先给四样（OEM）· 周四
**视觉**：彩盒 / 标签 / 打样单
**正文**：
For a private-label bathroom program, the first sample goes faster if the buyer sends four things: brand logo files, packaging artwork or dieline, the target carton drop test, and the label placement rules. Without them, we make a good product in the wrong box, and the second sample is really the first. We now ask for all four before we book sample room time.

What is on your pre-sample checklist?

#privatelabel #OEM #packaging

### 12. 工厂参观看什么（Trust）· 周六
**视觉**：来料检验区 / 返修台
**正文**：
On a factory tour, the most useful room is usually not the showroom. It is the incoming inspection area and the rework bench. The showroom shows what the factory can do on a good day; the inspection area shows what it catches on a normal day. We walk buyers through both, including the reject bin, because a factory that hides rework is telling you something.

What do you ask to see on a supplier visit?

#factorytour #qualitycontrol #sourcing
