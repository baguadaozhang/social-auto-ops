# English comment templates (primary language for overseas customers)
# Format: [tag] text    or plain text (no tag = general)
# Tags: supply / quality / cost / delivery / market / production / digital
# Hook variant: [tag,hook] = ends with a light, natural invitation
#   (spec sheet / checklist / "message me"). Keep hooks rare: 3 per topic group,
#   so roughly 1 in 4 comments carries an invitation and the rest stay discussion.
# Keep it conversational: questions and first hand experience beat generic praise.

# ---------- quality ----------

[quality] What defect rate are you running at? We have been pushing ours down for two years.

[quality] Quality cost and rework cost are really the same line item in the long run.
[quality] How do you set sampling levels? Too tight and cost climbs, too loose and risk climbs.
[quality] Agreed. Money spent on prevention is always cheaper than the fix afterwards.
[quality] We run first article inspection on every new mould, then AQL 1.0 in production. That combination caught almost everything before it shipped.
[quality] Glaze and shade matching across batches is our biggest QC headache. Two panels from different kiln loads can read as different colours under LED light.

[quality] Do you audit sub-suppliers yourself or rely on their certificates? We started annual visits and it changed our view of them.
[quality] The hardest defect to catch is the one that only shows up after installation. Water ingress on a shower tray is a good example.
[quality,hook] We keep a photo record per batch so containers can be compared later. If the folder structure we use would help, message me and I will send it.
[quality] We hold finished goods for three days before packing. It catches the defects that only show after the coating fully cures.
[quality] What is your acceptance rule for a minor scratch on a glossy panel? We keep arguing with one customer about what counts as minor.
[quality] Labelling is underrated QC. Wrong SKU labels on the carton cost more to fix than most production defects.
[quality,hook] We built a one page defect photo guide per product line so every inspector grades the same way; happy to send the format if it helps.
[quality] Do you leak test every piece or by batch? Ours is 100% on shower trays and it is expensive.
[quality] Packaging is part of quality. If the carton fails, the product does not matter to the buyer.
[quality] We measure first pass yield weekly. It moved more when we fixed the paint booth than when we added inspectors.
[quality] How do you handle field returns when the defect is cosmetic? We are still improvising on that one.
